About

I started my career in 2001 at the ITEM Progress cooperative, eventually joining the board of directors. Over twenty years I grew as a developer across web and mobile, maintaining an open and curious approach.

I worked as a research fellow, traveling across Europe and presenting at CARS in Berlin. I published academic work, worked in commercial settings as a developer, technology scout, and R&D lead — always balancing code quality with team building, cooperation, and the business side of every solution.

In recent years I have focused on PHP and Laravel, building applications and APIs for international clients — especially in automotive. I also spent several years in medical robotics with Surgica Robotica and Solinfo.

My goal is to design and implement modern, technologically advanced solutions built to solid standards, preferably with an agile mindset. I am increasingly interested in distributed, high-reliability systems with Erlang/Elixir/OTP and Phoenix, moving from single-threaded development toward SOA and multi-machine setups.
